# Half-twist neighborhood geometry: early versus delayed petals

This research-only audit follows Richard's clarity-first priority. It compares
already reached disk routes; it applies no new substitution and changes no
preferred diagram, payload, PDF or mathematical proof.

## What is measured

For every retained state and atomic neighbor, take the CLOSED neighborhood of
each remaining unit half-twist arc. Compute exact minimal disk intersections
for every unordered factor-record pair incident to such a neighborhood. Each
pair is counted once and twist powers are not multiplicities. These are NOT
arc intersection counts or a jointly realizable minimum drawing. The sum also
depends on how many factor records have been expanded.

The report retains the full closed-curve normal-form certificates. Each actual
half word stays explicit; its closed-neighborhood twist is checked to equal the
half squared. Full nonabelian last-strand kernels, shadows and reconstructions
are retained for these neighborhoods. No half permutation or kernel is
forgotten. Exhaustion raises; it never becomes a zero intersection count.

routes, not an exclusion of other transports.

The delayed daisy avoids introducing three petal diagrams while the last two
half twists are still being handled. Its lower aggregate sum partly reflects
this smaller record count; it does not prove the arcs themselves are less
tangled. It is a concrete clarity candidate with ONE MORE operation, not a
shortening. Its full input/output join and every ordered disk transition are
checked. The saved local daisy witness is retained under its original source
reference; it is not inferred from the whole-product action.
