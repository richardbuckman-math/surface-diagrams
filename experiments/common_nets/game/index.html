How to play

Fold the cool-colored net into the warm-colored target. Select a hinge from the list or double-click its line on the net. Its first key increases the angle; the second decreases it. Hold a key to keep folding. The last four hinges on the largest nets use Shift with their letter pairs. The slider, +/− buttons and mouse wheel also work.

With snapping on, keys, buttons and the wheel step between valid crease angles. The slider moves continuously and snaps when released near a valid crease. These angles come from how many tetrahedra meet around an edge. Target orientation, panel assignment and mirror images do not affect success. Dragging a view rotates the camera; it does not change the folds.

Progress and unfinished folds are saved in this browser. Showing a solution does not earn a solved mark; Start flat lets you try again. Hinges are ideal mathematical joints: this prototype checks folded end shapes and allows panels to pass through one another during folding. It does not simulate collision-free paper assembly.
