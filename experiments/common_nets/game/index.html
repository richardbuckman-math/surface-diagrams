How to play

Fold the cool-colored net into the warm-colored target. Select a hinge from the list or double-click its line on the net. Its first key increases the angle; the second decreases it. Hold a key to keep folding. Hinges 14–26 use Shift with their letter pairs; 27–33 use number or symbol pairs. The slider, +/− buttons and mouse wheel also work.

With snapping on, keys, buttons and the wheel step between crease angles. Cubes use quarter turns; tetrahedra use their own dihedral angles. The slider moves continuously and snaps when released near a valid crease. Target orientation and panel assignment do not affect success. Tetrahedron mirrors count together; chiral cube targets are separate. Dragging a view rotates the camera; it does not change the folds.

Progress and unfinished folds are saved in this browser. Showing a solution does not earn a solved mark; Start flat lets you try again. Hinges are ideal mathematical joints: this prototype checks folded end shapes and allows panels to pass through one another during folding. It does not simulate collision-free paper assembly. Download net SVG for a flat template: cut black/red edges and fold blue dashed edges.

Only the target list belonging to a net is claimed. Loading a family prepares its own puzzle collection.
