Three-point exercise

Start with one three-point boundary twist. Substitute the marked-point lantern relation to get three pairwise twists. Split the first pairwise twist into two half twists; drag the second half twist down one row, then drag the intervening pairwise twist down one row. The last two twists now combine into a square. The walkthrough button records every checked step so you can inspect or undo it.

The library currently offers power splitting, a two-point Dehn twist as two half twists, and the three-point marked-disk lantern substitution. The proposed conversion of a marked point into framed inner boundaries changes the surface; daisy and rose substitutions will appear here when their framing and factor order have been encoded and verified.
