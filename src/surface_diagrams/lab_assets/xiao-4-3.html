Conventions and current limits

Read factor rows and braid from top to bottom. Drag downward applies (u,v) → (u v u⁻¹,u); drag upward applies (v,u) → (u,u⁻¹ v u). Each adjacent move is checked using exact Artin actions. Global conjugation replaces every factor f by g f g⁻¹, changing the whole product by conjugation; the saved global frame records g through undo and reload.

Supports use the library’s Arc/Loop surface renderer. Arcs and closed curves are recovered from exact boundary words. Both must pass an exact class check and the library route solver. These are not verified reconstructions of the PDF. The seven normalized words use Xiao's (4,3) seed, with involution corrections for the two 3+3 branch factors. Every edit is checked against the seed's exact disk product, which is not the disk identity. Blue dotted lines separate words in one continuous braid.

The lantern option is for three marked points in a disk. Boundary-framed lantern and general daisy substitutions are not implemented. Combine joins equal adjacent powers or reverses a verified three-factor marked-point lantern. Long words may reach prototype limits; a rejected operation leaves the state unchanged.

    Sphere-action certificate

    Impose the sphere boundary relation D = x₁x₂x₃x₄x₅x₆ = 1. The resulting action is on the free group generated by x₁,…,x₅. When every meridian image is w · xᵢ · w⁻¹ for one common w, the sphere outer action is the identity. This check uses the current complete product; it does not claim that the six-strand disk braid is the identity. The seeded closed genus-two relation also uses a separate +I homology check.
