Sphere-action certificate

Impose the sphere boundary relation D = x₁x₂x₃x₄x₅x₆ = 1. The resulting action is on the free group generated by x₁,…,x₅. If every meridian image is w · xᵢ · w⁻¹ for one common w (and x₆ uses the boundary relation), the induced outer action is the identity. This checks the entire product, independently of the displayed cut-system routes. It does not distinguish the central order-two element from identity in the spherical braid group; the separate sphere-relation derivation addresses that stronger claim.
